**Republic Savings**

A budgeting app for people who want to take real control of their money. Built for Android, Republic Savings is designed around the South African cost of living, where every rand counts and knowing where it went matters.
Most budgeting apps connect to your bank and log everything automatically. We chose a different approach: you log your own expenses. It keeps you accountable, and it keeps you aware of your spending habits.

A demonstration and user starter guide can be located on youtube at: https://youtu.be/q3Tn3V3xsxw

Set a budget that fits your life. Create weekly, monthly or annual budgets and watch your progress as you spend. The app shows what percentage you have used, how much you have left, and a recommended daily amount to help you stay on track.
Log expenses your way. Use Express entry when you are in a hurry (just the amount and category), or In-depth entry when you want to add a description, the place you shopped, the date and even a photo of the receipt.
Organise with categories. Use the default categories or create your own, then tap a category to see everything you have spent in it.
Search your spending. Filter your expenses by date range, category or amount to see exactly where your money is going.
Earn rewards at The Fair. Stay under your daily or weekly limits and you earn a ticket. Spend it at The Fair for a chance to win a small treat you can enjoy without breaking your budget, from a pizza night to a whale watching tour.
Keep it private. Sign in with a password or biometrics. The app supports multiple users on the same device, and each person's data stays private to them. If you forget your password, account recovery has you covered.
Make it yours. Switch between light, dark or system theme, and choose your default currency.

**Getting started**
  Open the app and create an account.
  Set your currency and, if you like, enable biometric sign-in.
  Create your first budget and choose a time period.
  Tap the + button on the home screen whenever you spend something.
The video guide above walks through all of this.

**How we built it**
Republic Savings was built in Android Studio using Kotlin and Java, with RoomDB for local data storage, that supports multiple different users on the same device, with private and customizable data.
We used GitHub to host the project and collaborate as a team. Each of us worked on our own branches, changes were merged through pull requests, and we kept regular, clearly described commits so the project history stays easy to follow. This kept everyone's work in sync and helped us catch problems before they reached the main branch.
